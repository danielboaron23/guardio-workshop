## Design System — status: PARTIAL
- Tokens: `packages/tokens/src/index.ts` (shared with web). In the app: `apps/mobile/src/constants/theme.ts` (`theme`, `textStyle()`, `font()`). In the HTML concepts: `docs/todos-mobile/tokens.css`, generated by `docs/todos-mobile/export.mts`.
- Components (`apps/mobile/src/components/`):
  - `ui.tsx`: `Card` (radius lg, border), `SectionTitle` (title-lg), `PrimaryButton`, `Badge` (grey pill with a 12px icon).
  - `app-tabs.tsx`: floating tab bar (Home, My Assets, To do's, Activity, Account), with a red dot on To do's.
  - `home/`: hero per user state, status card (setup / pending to-dos), sections.
  - `icon.tsx` + `constants/assets.ts`: SVG assets by key.
- Icons: `packages/assets/home` (`m-*`, `tab-*`, `statusbar-*`, badge icons). Status-bar icons are white (drawn for the coloured hero).

## Output contract
- App: plain `StyleSheet` + `theme`; Expo Go-compatible libraries only; no web components.
- HTML concepts: `docs/todos-mobile/` — `mobile.css` (shell and parts), `app.js` (state, frame, sheet, controls), `concept-<x>.js`, `<x>.html`. Regenerate tokens, data and assets with `node docs/todos-mobile/export.mts`.

## Patterns & Conventions
- Page background `canvas`, white `Card`s, 20pt screen padding, a floating tab bar with a fade behind it. Content pads its bottom for the tab bar.

## Constraints
- WCAG AA. `textSecondary` on `canvas` is 4.55:1, just above the line: don't put it on anything darker.
- Touch targets ≥ 44pt; primary actions low on the screen but clear of the tab bar.

## Gaps & Opportunities
- No sheet, no tabs and no list rows in the app yet (the To-dos concepts add them in HTML).
- The To do's tab dot is always red; the concepts propose a count, with red only for something urgent.
